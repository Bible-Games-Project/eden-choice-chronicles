# Replace Rich Man and Lazarus character sprites

## Scope
Replace only the reusable character portraits used by **The Rich Man and Lazarus**. Preserve all story text, translations, choices, biblical answers, feedback, backgrounds, gameplay, UI, other stories, and all 22 existing scene keys.

## Portrait set
Ten reusable transparent portraits, using completed Stories 57–74 as direct visual references:
- Jesus introducing the parable
- the rich man feasting in fine clothing
- the same rich man turning away indifferently
- Lazarus weak and suffering
- Lazarus resting with one or two calm street dogs beside him
- Lazarus peaceful at Abraham's side
- the rich man in distress after death
- the same rich man pleading with lifted hands
- Abraham speaking firmly and compassionately
- Jesus closing the teaching

Each portrait is reused across its matching consequence scenes rather than one image per slide. Gates, houses, flames, tables and landscape stay in the existing backgrounds.

## Composition and style
- Exactly three waist-up: Jesus introducing, the rich man turning away, Jesus closing. The other seven end naturally at the knees; never show feet.
- Complete heads, hair, arms, elbows, hands and body sides inside comfortable transparent margins.
- Portrait-oriented 928×1152 PNGs, clean alpha edges, character only, no ground, scenery or shadows.
- Keep the established Jesus identity unchanged; match the exact rendering, proportions, facial design, lighting, shading, colors and clothing treatment of Stories 57–74.
- Keep the rich man, Lazarus and Abraham identical across all their portraits. Poverty, death and afterlife suffering stay non-graphic and family-friendly.

## Installation and validation
- Remove every old PNG/WebP portrait from this story and install only the ten final local PNGs.
- Update only this story's portrait imports, preserving all 22 scene keys and assignments.
- Inspect every portrait for identity continuity, anatomy, transparency, margins and framing.
- Confirm all 12 language files remain untouched, then verify the web build.
- Do not run native iOS or Android sync; that remains a manual step.
