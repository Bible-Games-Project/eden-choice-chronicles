# Replace Story 72 character sprites

## Scope
Replace only the reusable character portraits used by **The Lord’s Prayer**. Preserve all story text, translations, choices, feedback, backgrounds, gameplay, UI, other stories, and all 18 existing scene keys.

## Implementation
- Use the completed portraits from Stories 57–71 as direct visual references, especially the established adult Jesus, Peter, and disciple groups.
- Create seven new reusable transparent PNG portraits: disciples asking Jesus; Jesus teaching “Our Father”; Jesus teaching daily trust; Jesus teaching forgiveness; Jesus teaching mercy toward others; Jesus teaching deliverance and prayer; and Jesus with reflective, prayerful disciples for the private-prayer ending.
- Keep Jesus’s established identity, clothing, proportions, rendering, and coloring unchanged. Keep disciples historically appropriate, distinct, attentive, and consistent with earlier stories.
- Frame exactly three portraits waist-up and the other four knee-up. Show no feet, scenery, floors, environmental objects, or shadows; retain complete heads, hair, arms, elbows, hands, and comfortable transparent margins.
- Remove every old Story 72 PNG/WebP portrait, install only the seven final local PNGs, and update only Story 72’s portrait imports and assignments.

## Validation
- Inspect every portrait for exact style matching, identity continuity, natural anatomy, transparency, clean edges, margins, and correct waist/knee framing.
- Confirm all 18 Story 72 scene keys retain an appropriate reusable portrait and no other story files or assets changed.
- Verify image dimensions and alpha bounds, validate all 12 language files remain untouched and consistent, and run the web build.
