# Replace Story 75 character sprites

## Scope
Replace only the reusable character portraits used by **The Parable of the Sower**. Preserve all story text, translations, choices, biblical answers, feedback, backgrounds, gameplay, UI, other stories, and all 17 existing scene keys.

## Portrait set
Create eight reusable transparent PNG portraits, using completed Stories 57–74 as direct visual references:
- Jesus introducing the parable to the listeners
- the sower walking and scattering seed from a shoulder bag
- the same sower glancing toward the hard path where seed was lost
- the same sower with a concerned look at shallow, rocky ground
- the same sower drawing back from thorny growth
- the same sower joyfully holding a full ripe sheaf of grain
- disciples asking Jesus what the parable means
- Jesus explaining the soils and closing the lesson with the disciples

Reuse each portrait across the matching consequence scenes rather than one image per slide. Soils, seed, birds, plants and thorns stay in the existing backgrounds; only the small amount of grain a character naturally holds appears on a sprite.

## Composition and style
- Frame exactly three portraits waist-up: Jesus introducing the parable, the sower at the rocky ground, and Jesus explaining the meaning.
- Frame the other five knee-up, ending naturally at the knees. Never show feet.
- Keep complete heads, hair, arms, elbows, hands and body sides inside comfortable transparent margins.
- Produce portrait-oriented 928×1152 PNGs with clean alpha edges, character only, no ground, field, scenery or shadows.
- Keep the established Jesus identity unchanged and match the exact rendering, proportions, facial design, lighting, shading, colors and clothing treatment of Stories 57–74.
- Keep the sower's face, hair, beard and clothing identical across all five of his portraits, and the disciples consistent with earlier stories.

## Installation and validation
- Remove every old PNG/WebP portrait from Story 75 and install only the eight final local PNGs.
- Update only Story 75's portrait imports while preserving all 17 scene keys and assignments.
- Inspect every portrait for identity continuity, anatomy, transparency, margins, correct waist/knee framing and absence of feet or environment.
- Confirm all 12 language files remain untouched, then verify the web build.
- Do not run native iOS or Android sync; that remains a manual step.
