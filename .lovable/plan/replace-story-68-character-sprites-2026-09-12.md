# Replace Story 68 character sprites

## Scope
Replace only the six reusable character portraits used by **Walking on Water**. Preserve all story content, translations, backgrounds, gameplay, UI, other stories, and scene-to-sprite assignments.

## Implementation
- Use the completed New Testament portraits as direct visual references for Jesus, Peter, and the disciples.
- Create six new transparent portrait PNGs matching the existing reusable roles:
  - disciples rowing/struggling
  - disciples frightened by the figure
  - Jesus walking and reassuring
  - Peter stepping out in faith
  - Peter sinking and reaching for help
  - Jesus rescuing Peter
- Frame exactly three portraits waist-up and three knee-up; include no feet, boat, water, scenery, floor, or shadows.
- Preserve recurring identities, especially Jesus and Peter, across every portrait.
- Remove the old Story 68 PNG/WebP files, install only the final PNG set, and update only the Story 68 sprite imports.

## Validation
- Inspect every portrait for style consistency, anatomy, transparency, clean margins, and correct framing.
- Confirm all 18 Story 68 scenes retain their existing reusable mappings.
- Verify image dimensions/alpha bounds and run the app build.
- Do not run native iOS or Android sync; that remains a manual step.
