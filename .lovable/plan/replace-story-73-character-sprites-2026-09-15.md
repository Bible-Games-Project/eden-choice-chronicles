# Replace Story 73 character sprites

## Scope
Replace only the reusable character portraits used by **The Good Samaritan**. Preserve all story text, translations, choices, biblical answers, feedback, backgrounds, gameplay, UI, other stories, and all 20 existing scene keys.

## Portrait set
Create nine reusable transparent PNG portraits, using completed Stories 57–72 as direct visual references:
- Jesus introducing the parable
- the traveler confronted and frightened by a small, restrained group of robbers
- the same traveler wounded, shown safely and without graphic injury
- the priest passing by
- the Levite passing by
- the Samaritan discovering the traveler and showing compassion
- the Samaritan treating the same traveler
- the Samaritan, recovering traveler, and innkeeper completing the care arrangement
- Jesus calmly delivering the final “go and do likewise” lesson

Reuse each portrait across the matching consequence scenes rather than creating one image per slide. Keep recurring faces, hair, clothing, age, proportions, and skin tone unchanged throughout.

## Composition and style
- Frame exactly three portraits waist-up: Jesus introducing the parable, the priest, and the Levite. Frame the other six knee-up.
- Never show feet. Keep complete heads, hair, arms, elbows, hands, and body sides inside comfortable transparent margins.
- Produce portrait-oriented 928×1152 PNGs with clean alpha edges and no scenery, road, inn, animal, floor, environment, or shadows.
- Match the established Jesus identity and the exact rendering, proportions, facial design, lighting, shading, colors, clothing treatment, and silhouettes of Stories 57–72.
- Keep violence non-graphic and family-friendly; robbers remain believable first-century men rather than exaggerated villains.

## Installation and validation
- Remove every old PNG/WebP portrait from Story 73 and install only the nine final local PNGs.
- Update only Story 73’s portrait imports and assignments while preserving all 20 scene keys.
- Inspect every portrait for identity continuity, anatomy, transparency, clean margins, exact waist/knee framing, and absence of feet or environmental elements.
- Confirm all 12 language files remain untouched and consistent, then verify the web build and preview status.
- Do not run native iOS or Android sync; that remains a manual step.
