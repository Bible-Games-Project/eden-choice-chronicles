# Replace Story 74 character sprites

## Scope
Replace only the reusable character portraits used by **The Prodigal Son**. Preserve all story text, translations, choices, biblical answers, feedback, backgrounds, gameplay, UI, other stories, and all 25 existing scene keys.

## Portrait set
Create nine reusable transparent PNG portraits, using completed Stories 57–73 as direct visual references:
- Jesus introducing the parable
- the younger son confidently asking his father for his inheritance (with the father)
- the younger son leaving home, confident and eager
- the younger son in careless wealth in the distant land
- the younger son poor and desperate among the pigs, shown without squalor detail
- the younger son resolving to return, ashamed but hopeful
- the father running to embrace the returning son
- the father celebrating with a servant preparing the welcome
- the older son upset, with the father speaking compassionately
- Jesus closing with the father's joy lesson

Reuse each portrait across its matching consequence scenes rather than one image per slide. Keep recurring faces, hair, beard, age, clothing, proportions, and skin tone unchanged; the younger son's clothing may grow worn while his identity stays recognizable.

## Composition and style
- Frame exactly three portraits waist-up: Jesus introducing the parable, the younger son among the pigs, and Jesus closing. Frame the rest knee-up.
- Never show feet. Keep complete heads, hair, arms, elbows, hands, and body sides inside comfortable transparent margins.
- Produce portrait-oriented 928×1152 PNGs with clean alpha edges and no scenery, house, field, pigs, floor, environment, or shadows.
- Match the established Jesus identity and the exact rendering, proportions, facial design, lighting, shading, colors, clothing treatment, and silhouettes of Stories 57–73.
- Keep hardship non-graphic and family-friendly.

## Installation and validation
- Remove every old PNG/WebP portrait from Story 74 and install only the final local PNGs.
- Update only Story 74's portrait imports and assignments while preserving all 25 scene keys.
- Inspect every portrait for identity continuity, anatomy, transparency, margins, exact waist/knee framing, and absence of feet or environmental elements.
- Confirm all 12 language files remain untouched, then verify the web build.
- Do not run native iOS or Android sync; that remains a manual step.
